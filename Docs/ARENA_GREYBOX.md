# INDIAN FIRE world map

The old square greybox arena is replaced in the scene builder by an original, stylized India-inspired island map. It is a fictional game world, not a precise geographic or navigational representation.

## Included named locations

- Snowcrest Gate — Himalayan-inspired highlands
- Victory Arch — capital-region landmark
- Moonstone Palace — desert heritage-inspired district
- Liongate Port — western coastal district
- Four Arch Bazaar — old-city-inspired market district
- River Crown — eastern river-region district
- Golden Sanctuary — northwestern heritage-inspired landmark
- Sunspire Coast — eastern coastal district
- Tech Valley — southern city district
- Lotus Reach — central-southern district
- Backwater Haven — southwestern coastal district

These are original in-game names inspired by broad Indian cultural and landscape themes. The map does not reproduce exact real-world streets, buildings, or tactical layouts.

## Build

1. Open the project in Unity.
2. Select **Tools → Battle Royale → Build Training Scene**.
3. Press Play. The map is generated by `IndianFireWorldMap`.
4. The Unity scene builder still creates the existing practice controls and training bots.

The world is a procedural prototype made from primitive terrain tiles and simple landmark markers. Replace these with original art and optimize the terrain before release.
